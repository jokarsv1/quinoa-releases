# Quinoa for Mac

Private dictation and meeting notes that run entirely on your Mac. Hold a key, speak, and your words appear wherever your cursor is.

## Download

**[Download Quinoa.dmg](https://github.com/jokarsv1/quinoa-releases/releases/latest/download/Quinoa.dmg)** · Website: https://jokarsv1.github.io/quinoa-releases

Requires a Mac with Apple Silicon (M1 or newer) and macOS 14.2 or later.

## Install

1. Open the DMG and drag **Quinoa** into **Applications**.
2. Open Quinoa. macOS will say it can't verify the developer. Click **Done**.
3. Go to **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll down, and click **Open Anyway** next to Quinoa. You only do this once.
4. Follow Quinoa's setup: Microphone, Input Monitoring, and Accessibility.

The first launch downloads the speech model (about 450 MB, one time).

## Updates

Quinoa checks for a new version once a day and shows a banner on its Home screen. Download the new DMG and replace the app in Applications. Your settings and permissions stay.

## Privacy

Speech is transcribed on your Mac. Audio and text are never uploaded. The only network requests are the one-time model download, the daily update check and, only if you opt in, anonymous word corrections. Read the [privacy policy](PRIVACY.md).
