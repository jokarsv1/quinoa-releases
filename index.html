What it does

Talk naturally. Get clean text.

Quinoa understands the way people actually speak: pauses, “umm”, changing your mind, and switching between Hindi and English.

Types in any app

Mail, Slack, WhatsApp, Docs, your code editor. Hold the key, speak, let go. The words land at your cursor.

Private by design

Speech is turned into text on your Mac. Your voice and your words are never uploaded. Works offline.

Cleans up as you go

Drops fillers, keeps your final thought when you correct yourself, and turns spoken lists into bullet points.

“meet at 2, no wait, 3” → meet at 3

Knows Hinglish

Comes with everyday words like yaar, bhai, accha and theek hai, plus names like UPI, Paytm and Aadhaar.

“thik hai bhaai” → theek hai bhai

Learns your words

Fix a name once and Quinoa asks to remember it. Next time it gets it right.

Meeting notes

Record any call: Zoom, Meet, Teams. Get a transcript that separates what you said from what others said.

Install

Up and running in two minutes

Quinoa is a free direct download, not from the App Store, so macOS asks you to confirm it once.

  1. Download Quinoa.dmg, open it, and drag Quinoa into Applications.
  2. Open Quinoa. macOS says it can't verify the developer. Click Done.
  3. Go to System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click Open Anyway. You only do this once.
  4. Follow the setup: allow the microphone, Input Monitoring for the hold key, and Accessibility for typing. The speech model downloads once (about 450 MB).
